Break your videos beautifully.

moshpit is a video editor with a datamosher built in. Cut your clips together, then melt, bloom and glitch them by editing the compressed video itself. Free, open source, and nothing is uploaded.

A real datamosher, inside a real editor.

Most glitch apps paint effects over the picture. moshpit rewrites the compressed frames themselves, the way datamoshing was always done, with a timeline and a live preview around it.

Melts and blooms

Remove keyframes so one shot melts into the next, repeat motion until the picture erupts, borrow movement from another clip, or corrupt the bytes.

Multiple video and audio tracks, trimming, splitting, titles, keyframes, speed ramps, chroma key and LUTs, with mosh effects as bars you drag and resize.

Questions

What is datamoshing?

Video files mostly store changes, not pictures: a full picture now and then (a keyframe) and in between only how blocks of it move. Remove a keyframe and the movement of the next shot gets applied to the old picture, so it melts. Repeat a movement and the picture smears and blooms. Read more.

Is it really free? Are my videos uploaded?

Yes, and no. moshpit is free software (GPL-3.0). Your videos never leave your device: decoding, moshing and encoding all happen in your browser or in the desktop app.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Phones get their own editor with a big preview, a swipe-to-scrub timeline and tools that follow what you select. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

How long and how big can videos be?

In the browser, up to 10 minutes and comfortably 1080p; 1440p and 4K work for short videos. The desktop app goes to 30 minutes and is much faster at 4K.

Why does the exported AVI look different in every player?

Because a moshed AVI is broken on purpose and every player hides the damage in its own way. MP4, WebM and GIF exports contain exactly what the preview shows.

Can I help?

Please do. Report bugs, suggest features or send code on GitHub, and share what you make in the gallery.
