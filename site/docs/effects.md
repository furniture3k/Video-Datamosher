# Effects

**Mosh effects** change the compressed video, so the decoder rebuilds the picture from damaged instructions. **Picture effects** run on the decoded frames, after the mosh. Each effect sits on the timeline as a bar: move it and drag its ends to choose where it acts.

Effects that repeat or insert frames have a **Timing** setting. *Keep length* (the default) keeps the picture, the sound and the playhead in sync; *Stretch* makes the video longer instead.
