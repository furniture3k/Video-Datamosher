# moshpit docs

moshpit is a video editor with a datamosher built in. You cut clips, music and titles together on a timeline, then mosh the result by editing the compressed video itself. It runs in your browser (and installs from there) or as a desktop app, and nothing is uploaded.

- **[Getting started](./getting-started/)**: your first moshed video in a few minutes.
- **[What is datamoshing?](./what-is-datamoshing/)**: why videos melt, and what each kind of damage looks like.
- **[Effects](./effects/)**: every mosh and picture effect and its settings.
- **[Transitions](./transitions/)**: the mosh transitions (melts) and the classic ones.
- **[Sharing and exporting](./sharing/)**: formats, recipes, and the gallery.
