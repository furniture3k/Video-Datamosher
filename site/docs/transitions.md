# Transitions

Click the **+** on a cut, or drag a transition from the **Transitions** tab onto it. Drag the transition's band to change how long it lasts.

**Mosh transitions** last exactly as long as their band, then a fresh keyframe brings the next shot back clean (turn **Resolve at the end** off to let the melt run on). **Classic transitions** blend the two shots.
